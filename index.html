About Seven Starr Trucking

A Logistics Partner You Can Depend On

Seven Starr Trucking LLC was founded with a vision to provide dependable, efficient, and customer-focused freight transportation services across the United States. Starting with a commitment to integrity, safety, and operational excellence, the company has built strong relationships with brokers, shippers, and drivers alike.

Common Inquiries

Logistics & Compliance FAQ

Find fast answers regarding driver onboarding, lane coverage, and shipping dispatch estimates.

What geographic areas does Seven Starr Trucking serve?

We provide nationwide logistics coverage, hauling freight across all 48 continental states. Our primary dispatch terminal is based in Hialeah, Florida (Miami area), with heavy lane density in the East Coast, Midwest, and Southern regions.

What specific types of freight do you haul?

Our fleet is fully equipped with 53ft Dry Van trailers. We haul a wide range of dry commodities, including palletized dry goods, consumer products, industrial parts, paper items, and non-hazardous general freight.

How quickly can I receive a shipping lane quote?

Shippers or brokers submitting quotes via our website form generally receive a custom contract rate sheet directly back in their email inbox within 30 to 60 minutes during standard dispatch operating hours.

Do you work with third-party logistics brokers?

Yes, we maintain carrier accounts and setups with major logistics brokers like CH Robinson, TQL, Coyote Logistics, and Echo Global. We are fully bonded and hold active USDOT #4009071 and MC #1689367 authorities.

Can independent Owner Operators lease onto your MC?

Yes! We have a leasing program for independent owner operators. You can submit your CDL, insurance documents, and truck details directly through our application form. We offer weekly direct settlements, compliance assistance, and high-paying dispatch options.
